## 🎥 STYLE GUIDE — THE AEVY TV FORMULA

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **This is NOT a traditional "screen recording with someone talking" video.**
> This is a cinematic documentary. Here's how it works:
>
> 1. **Voiceover narration** runs continuously — the speaker is almost NEVER on camera (except the intro and close)
> 2. **B-roll clips** (AI-generated or stock footage) play underneath the narration — smoke, factories, inspectors walking, drone shots, city skylines
> 3. **Screen recordings** of the actual product are layered in seamlessly — they appear when the narrator says "let me show you" and disappear when the narration goes back to storytelling
> 4. **Text overlays** animate onto screen to emphasize key phrases — "6 to 8 weeks" slams onto screen in bold
> 5. **Music** builds and drops with the emotional arc — tense during the problem, upbeat during the demo, inspirational at the close

### Tools You'll Need:
- **CapCut** (free, easy) or **DaVinci Resolve** (free, professional) for editing
- **AI video clips**: Use Pika, Runway ML, or Kling AI to generate 5-10 second cinematic clips
- **Stock footage**: Pexels.com or Pixabay.com (both free, no attribution needed)
- **Screen recorder**: OBS Studio (free) for recording your ServiceNow demo
- **Music**: YouTube Audio Library (royalty-free)

## Cutting from 15 → 10 minutes (Priority Order)

| Priority | What to Cut | Saves |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | Scene 5.3 (Security roles) — compress to 15 seconds of text overlays with no voiceover | ~45 sec |
| 2nd | Scene 1.4 (Architecture diagram) — merge into Scene 1.3, flash for 5 sec | ~20 sec |
| 3rd | Shorten Scene 3.1 (Python code) — skip the code scrolling, use only the animated flowchart | ~30 sec |
| 4th | Shorten Scene 2.2 (Form) — speed up the screen recording to 1.5x | ~30 sec |
| 5th | Shorten Scene 5.1 (Legal) — reduce to 20 seconds, just flash the record | ~25 sec |
| 6th | Tighten Scene 4.2 (Mobile) — cut inspector GPS proof section | ~20 sec |
| 7th | Speed up ALL screen recordings to 1.25x (barely noticeable) | ~60 sec |

> **Total savings: ~3.5 min → brings you from ~14 min to ~10.5 min**

## Music Guide

| Timestamp | Mood | Suggestion |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 – 2:00 | Tense, documentary | YouTube Audio Library → "Documentary Tension" or "Ambient Dark" |
| 2:00 – 7:00 | Upbeat, modern tech | YouTube Audio Library → "Tech Innovation" or "Digital Future" |
| 7:00 – 10:00 | Confident, operational | YouTube Audio Library → "Corporate Drive" |
| 10:00 – 12:00 | Serious, authoritative | YouTube Audio Library → "News Report Background" |
| 12:00 – 14:00 | Inspirational crescendo | YouTube Audio Library → "Inspiring Corporate" or "Rising Hope" |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Music volume: 10-15% MAX.** Barely audible. The voiceover is the star. Music is just emotional seasoning.

## Text Overlay Style Guide

- **Font:** Inter, Montserrat, or Poppins (clean, modern)
- **Color:** White text with subtle drop shadow on dark backgrounds. Dark green (#0a3a22) text on light backgrounds.
- **Animation:** Fade in over 0.5 seconds. Never bounce, spin, or fly in.
- **Position:** Lower-third for labels. Center screen for impact statements.
- **Duration:** On screen for 3-4 seconds minimum — give the audience time to read.

## Final Tips

> [!CAUTION]
> **DO NOT show faces during the demo sections.** This is the #1 difference from a normal hackathon video. In Aevy TV style, the voice is disembodied — it feels like a documentary narrator, not someone talking into their webcam. The only face shots should be in the intro skit (B3, B4) and the team introduction (B12).

> [!TIP]
> **The secret to making this feel cinematic:** Never let a single shot last more than 8 seconds. Quick cuts between B-roll and screen recordings, timed to the narration, create energy and keep the judges engaged. Even during screen recordings, zoom in and out slowly to create motion.
